# How Broadcasts Work

When an event's restream target is a YouTube channel, the platform creates one YouTube broadcast
per **broadcast day** — a date in the event's timezone. Each broadcast is its own YouTube video
with its own link.

- **Start Push** and **Go Live** always use **today's broadcast**: the current (not replaced)
  broadcast for today's date in the event's timezone.
- A running push stays on the broadcast it started with, even past midnight, until you stop it.
- With no broadcast for today, Start Push is refused with "No YouTube broadcast for today (*date*,
  *timezone*) -- create one on the event page". It never falls back to another day's broadcast or
  anything else.
- Changing the event's stream key doesn't affect its broadcasts.

See [[Event-Streaming-Management/Operators/3. Running a Live Event|Running a Live Event]] for
pushing and going live on the day.

# Before You Can Create a Broadcast

On the event configuration's **Livestream** tab, the **Restream target** must be "YouTube channel
(system-managed broadcasts)", with a **Livestream channel** and a **Start time** set. A Client
Admin sets these (see
[[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/6. Event Configuration Reference#Livestream|Livestream]]).
Until then, **Create** is dimmed with one of these reasons:

- "Set the restream target to 'YouTube channel' in the Livestream config section first"
- "Set a livestream start time in the Livestream config section first"
- "🔒 Turned off by system administrators"

# Adding Days and Creating a Broadcast

The **Broadcast days** section is on the event configuration's **Livestream** tab, and shows only
when the restream target is a YouTube channel. Adding days and creating broadcasts needs **User**
or above.

1. Pick a date and click **+ Add a day**. (Clicking it with no date picked shows "Pick a date
   first".) The day is added as **Day *n***.
2. Each day's row shows its current broadcast's status and a **watch** link to it on YouTube, or
   "No broadcast" if it has none yet.
3. Click **Create** on the row to create that day's broadcast on YouTube.

**Remove** deletes a day from the list. It's refused while that day's broadcast is live: "Can't
remove Day *n* while its YouTube broadcast is live".

# Replacing a Broadcast

A broadcast that has ended on YouTube can't receive video or go live again. When a day already has
a broadcast, its button reads **Replace** instead of **Create**. Replacing asks first, in a
**Replace broadcast?** dialog:

> "Create a replacement broadcast for Day *n*? It is a new YouTube video with a new link.
>
> Current broadcast: *watch link*
> Viewers with the announced link will not see the new broadcast.
>
> The current broadcast stays on YouTube; you can delete it afterwards from the broadcast
> history."

Click **Replace** to go ahead. What that means:

- The replacement is a **new YouTube video with a new link**.
- Viewers with the link you already announced won't see it.
- The old video stays on YouTube; nothing is deleted.
- A push that's already running stays on the old broadcast and errors with "This push's broadcast
  was replaced -- Stop and Start Push to switch to the new one". Stop Push, then Start Push.

Replacing is refused while the broadcast is live: "Can't replace Day *n*'s broadcast while it is
live on YouTube".

> [!warning]
> Replace only when the broadcast has ended (Go Live shows "This YouTube broadcast has ended —
> replace it to go live again") or was deleted on YouTube. Replacing doesn't fix a broadcast that
> looks healthy but won't play — see
> [[Event-Streaming-Management/Operators/6. Troubleshooting#YouTube Shows Healthy but Won't Play|YouTube Shows Healthy but Won't Play]].
> After you replace, tell whoever shared the old link.

# Replaced Broadcasts and Delete on YouTube

Replaced broadcasts stay listed under their day, labelled **Replaced**, with "*status* — watch",
or "deleted on YouTube *date*" once deleted.

**Delete on YouTube** on a replaced broadcast's row deletes its video from YouTube. It needs
**Client Admin**. It's permanent and may remove a recording people have watched. It confirms
first with a **Delete on YouTube?** dialog: "This permanently deletes the video *watch link* from
YouTube." and a **Delete on YouTube** button.

Only replaced broadcasts can be deleted here. The platform checks the broadcast's status on
YouTube first, and you may see:

- "This broadcast is still on air on YouTube; end it before deleting it"
- "Could not check this broadcast's status on YouTube, so it was not deleted: …"
- "YouTube has no such broadcast on this channel (already deleted, or the channel was reconnected
  under another account)"
- "YouTube does not allow deleting this broadcast in its current state"
- "This broadcast was already deleted on YouTube"

# What Broadcasts Lock

A broadcast is tied to the channel and settings it was created with, so broadcasts lock some of
the event's settings:

- While broadcasts are open (not yet ended), the **Restream target** and **Livestream channel**
  can't change: "Delete or complete the *N* current broadcast(s) first (*dates*) to change the
  restream mode or channel."
- Broadcast days from today on lock the event's timezone: "Broadcast days from today on (*dates*)
  are dates in this timezone. Remove them to change the timezone."
- A live broadcast or a running push locks the **Restream key** and **Privacy**, for example "The
  YouTube broadcast for *date* is live. Stop the broadcast to change where it goes (privacy)." or
  "The restream push is running. Stop the push to change where it goes (restream key)."

A locked field shows a 🔒 next to its label; hover over it to see why. See
[[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/6. Event Configuration Reference#Field Locks|Field Locks]]
for every lock.

# Who Can Do What

| Action | Needs |
|---|---|
| Add a day, **Create**, **Replace**, **Remove** | **User** or above |
| **Go Live**, **Stop Broadcast** | **User** or above |
| **Delete on YouTube** | **Client Admin** |
| Restream target, livestream channel, start time, privacy | **Client Admin** |

Members without the needed role see these controls dimmed with "🔒 Needs User or above" or "🔒
Needs Client Admin".
