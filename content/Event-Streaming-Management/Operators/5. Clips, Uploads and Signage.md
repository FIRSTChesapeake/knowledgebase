# Clips Are Automatic

Cutting and publishing clips is a pipeline that runs on its own as matches are detected during the
event — there's no button an operator clicks to "publish" a match. Your job here is reviewing what
came out the other end, not approving each clip before it goes out.

# Reviewing Clips

The Clips page lists clips across your events, with each clip's status and a link through to the
uploaded YouTube video. Use it after an event to spot-check that every match got a clip and
uploaded correctly.

# Uploads

The Uploads page shows upload history and status. If an upload shows as paused, it's usually one of
two things:

- **A system-wide quota limit** — YouTube limits how much the platform can upload per day across
  all events; a pause clears automatically once the quota resets. See
  [[Event-Streaming-Management/Operators/6. Troubleshooting#My Upload Is Stuck|Troubleshooting]].
- **The platform's current operation mode** — a system admin setting that can limit how much of the
  pipeline runs.

Either way, this isn't something an operator setting can fix directly — contact your system admin
(or *FIRST* Chesapeake technology support at
[technology@firstchesapeake.org](mailto:technology@firstchesapeake.org)) if uploads stay paused
longer than expected.

# Signage

If your event has signs assigned (pit, queue, audience, or wait-time displays, a custom URL, or the
livestream itself), assign and manage them from the Signage page. Each sign is tied to a stream
key and shows one display mode at a time; switch modes here rather than at the sign itself.
