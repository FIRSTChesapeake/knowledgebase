# What a Client Is

On this platform, your organization's account is called a **Client**. Everything under Client
Administration — members, keys, integrations, branding — is scoped to your Client; you won't see
another organization's data, and system-wide settings (covered in
[[Event-Streaming-Management/System-Administration/index|System Administration]]) are managed
separately by system admins, not by Client Admins.

# Member Roles

Every member of your Client has exactly one role, stored as `admin`, `user`, or `readonly`:

| Role | Shown as |
|---|---|
| `admin` | Admin — full access, can manage members and keys |
| `user` | User — can create events and view data |
| `readonly` | Readonly — view only |

Those descriptions are the exact text shown next to each option when you invite someone, and they're
a fair summary, but a few pages are more specifically **Client Admin only** — see the table below
before assuming "user" covers something.

# What Each Role Can Actually Do

| Page / action | Readonly | User | Admin |
|---|---|---|---|
| View Events, Clips, Uploads | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Run live controls (Start Push, Go Live, etc.) | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Configure a discovered event (Basics, Blue Alliance) | ❌ | ❌* | ✅ |
| Add/remove broadcast days | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Assign signage to an event | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manage Livestream config, thumbnail, Signage integration key | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| View Stream Keys (masked) | ❌ | ❌ | ✅** |
| Reveal/copy a real Stream Key value | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Create/change/delete a Stream Key | ❌ | ❌ | ❌*** |
| Create/delete a Restream Key | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Members: invite, change role, disable, remove | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| YouTube / Slack: link, unlink, configure notifications | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Branding (brand name, logo, colors) | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| OBS Plugin Devices: pair, revoke | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |

\* The Basics and Blue Alliance tabs on the Event Configuration page currently show enabled fields
and a Save button to a **user**-role member, but saving them actually requires Client Admin — a
user who tries will get a "Client admin access required" error, not a successful save. Don't rely
on this appearing to work; have a Client Admin do it.

\** Only the **Stream Keys** and **Restream Keys** pages themselves are Client-Admin-only in the
dashboard nav — a plain "user" or "readonly" member won't see either page listed at all.

\*** Creating, changing, or deleting a Stream Key is **system-admin only**, not Client Admin — see
[[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/2. Stream Keys and Restream Keys|Stream Keys and Restream Keys]]
for what a Client Admin can and can't do with stream keys versus restream keys.

# Inviting a Member

1. As a Client Admin, open **Members** in the dashboard.
2. Click **+ Invite Member**.
3. Fill in the **Email address** field (this is only used to identify the member — no invitation
   email is actually sent by the platform) and choose a **Role** from the dropdown: "Admin — full
   access, can manage members and keys," "User — can create events and view data," or "Readonly —
   view only."
4. Click **Send Invite**.
5. The panel switches to show an invite link under "Share this invite link with the new member,"
   with a **Copy Link** button. Copy it and send it to the person yourself (email, chat, however
   you'd normally reach them) — the platform does not email it for you. Click **Done** when
   finished.
6. The new member appears immediately in the Members table with a yellow **Pending** badge. If you
   need the link again later, a small **Copy invite** button appears next to their name (visible to
   Client Admins and system admins only) until they accept it.

Invite links expire after 7 days. If one expires before the person accepts it, invite them again.

# Changing a Member's Role

Each row in the Members table has an inline role dropdown. Pick a new role and confirm the dialog,
which reads exactly:

> Change {name}'s role to "{newRole}"?

You can't change your own role from this control — it's disabled on your own row. The platform also
won't let you demote the Client's last remaining admin (the request fails rather than leaving the
Client with no admin at all) — if you need to step down as the only admin, promote someone else to
Admin first.

# Disabling or Removing a Member

- **Disable** (button per row) turns off a member's ability to log in without deleting their
  account or history. Confirm text: "Disable {name}? They will no longer be able to log in." Click
  **Enable** on the same row later to restore access ("Enable {name}? They will be able to log in
  again.").
- **Delete** permanently removes their login access. Confirm text: "Delete {name}? This will
  permanently remove their login access." This is disabled on your own row — you can't delete your
  own account from here.
- As with role changes, you can't delete or disable your way down to zero admins on a Client — the
  last remaining admin is protected.
