# What a Client Is

On this platform, your organization's account is called a **Client**. Everything under Client
Administration — members, keys, integrations, branding — is scoped to your Client; you won't see
another organization's data, and system-wide settings (covered in
[[Event-Streaming-Management/System-Administration/index|System Administration]]) are managed
separately by system admins, not by Client Admins.

# Member Roles

Each member of your Client has exactly one role:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| `admin` | Everything below, plus Stream Keys, Restream Keys, YouTube/Slack integrations, Members, Branding, and OBS Plugin Devices |
| `user` | Everything `readonly` can do, plus Signage |
| `readonly` | View Events, Clips, and Uploads |

# Inviting a Member

An admin invites a new member by email from the Members page. The invitee receives an invite link
and sets up their own login when they accept it — you don't create or share a password for them.
