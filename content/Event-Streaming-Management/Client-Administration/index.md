[[#Guides in This Section]]

This guide is for administrators of your organization's account (called a **Client** on this
platform) — managing members and roles, connecting integrations, and setting organization-wide
defaults. If you're looking for day-of-event operation instead, see
[[Event-Streaming-Management/Operators/index|Operators]].
