# Contents
[[#Guides in This Section]]

This guide is for administrators of your organization's account (called a **Client** on this
platform) — managing members and roles, connecting integrations, and setting organization-wide
defaults. If you're looking for day-of-event operation instead, see
[[Event-Streaming-Management/Operators/index|Operators]].

The **YouTube**, **Slack**, **Members** and **Branding** pages appear in the menu only for Client
Admins. Every other page is visible to all members, with controls a role can't use dimmed.

# Guides in This Section

1. [[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/1. Members and Roles|Members and Roles]] —
   what a Client is, the three client roles (**Readonly**, **User**, **Client Admin**), what each
   can do, why a control shows 🔒, and inviting people.
2. [[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/2. Stream Keys and Restream Keys|Stream Keys and Restream Keys]] — viewing/revealing stream keys, and creating, rotating, and
   deleting restream keys.
3. [[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/3. YouTube and Slack Integrations|YouTube and Slack Integrations]] — connecting your YouTube channel and Slack workspace.
4. [[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/4. Branding|Branding]] — your Client's name,
   logo, and colors in the dashboard sidebar and on your signs' idle and waiting pages (not clip
   pre/post-roll — that's system-admin-managed).
5. [[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/5. OBS Plugin Devices|OBS Plugin Devices]] —
   downloading and installing the plugin, pairing and revoking devices.
6. [[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/6. Event Configuration Reference|Event Configuration Reference]] — a field-by-field reference for event setup: stream window,
   livestream target and broadcast days, locked fields.

Broadcast days themselves (adding days, creating and replacing YouTube broadcasts) are run by
**Users** on event day; the full procedure is in
[[Event-Streaming-Management/Operators/8. YouTube Broadcasts|YouTube Broadcasts]].
