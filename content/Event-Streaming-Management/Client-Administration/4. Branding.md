# What This Page Controls

The **Branding** page appears in the menu only for Client Admins. It controls your Client's name,
logo and colors in two places:

- the dashboard's own sidebar, for every member of your Client;
- your signs' idle page (shown when no event controls a sign) and the "Waiting for Livestream" page
  the Livestream display shows when nothing is live (see
  [[Event-Streaming-Management/Operators/5. Clips, Uploads and Signage#Signage|Clips, Uploads and Signage]]).

It does not touch clip videos. If you're looking for pre-roll/post-roll video branding on published
clips, see [[#Pre-Roll and Post-Roll Video (Not Here)|below]].

# Setting Your Branding

As a Client Admin, open **Branding** in the dashboard. There are four fields, with a live preview of
the sidebar next to the form:

- **Brand Name** — text, displayed in the sidebar header. Defaults to "CHS Streaming Services" in
  the dashboard if left blank; the sign pages fall back to your Client's name.
- **Logo URL** — a URL to an already-hosted image, not a file upload. Displayed above the brand name
  in the sidebar and on the sign pages; leave blank to show text only.
- **Accent Color** — a color picker plus a 6-digit hex text field (default `#cba6f7`). Used for
  active nav links, brand text, and highlights.
- **Sidebar Color** — a color picker plus a 6-digit hex text field (default `#1e1e2e`). Used for the
  sidebar background and the sign pages' background; the text color on top of it is computed
  automatically for contrast.

Click **Save**, which is disabled until you've changed something; a "Saved successfully" message
confirms it. An invalid hex value (not exactly `#` followed by 6 hex digits) is silently cleared,
falling back to the default color, rather than blocking Save — if a color doesn't look right after
saving, re-check the hex field.

# Pre-Roll and Post-Roll Video (Not Here)

Pre-roll and post-roll video branding on published clips are **not** configured from this page, and
a Client Admin can't upload either one directly:

- **Pre-roll** video (720p and 1080p, max 5 seconds each, up to 100 MB) is uploaded per-Client by a
  system admin. Once uploaded, a Client Admin can turn it on or off for an individual event using the
  **"Apply pre-roll branding video to clips from this event"** checkbox on that event's Basics tab —
  but only if a pre-roll asset already exists for your Client (the checkbox doesn't appear
  otherwise). The checkbox stays editable while the event is live. Users and Readonly members see it
  dimmed, with "🔒 Needs Client Admin" under the Basics title. See
  [[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/6. Event Configuration Reference#Basics|Event Configuration Reference]].
- **Post-roll** video is a single platform-wide asset a system admin uploads once (also 720p/1080p,
  up to 100 MB); it applies automatically to every clip on every Client with no per-event or
  per-Client toggle.

If your organization wants pre-roll footage uploaded or changed, contact *FIRST* Chesapeake
technology support at [technology@firstchesapeake.org](mailto:technology@firstchesapeake.org).
