# Linking a YouTube Channel

1. As a Client Admin, open **YouTube Channels** in the dashboard.
2. Click **+ Link Channel**. This takes you to Google's sign-in and consent screen (real OAuth, not
   a form on this platform) — sign in with the Google account that owns the YouTube channel you want
   to connect, and approve the requested permissions.
3. You're redirected back to the dashboard with a green banner: "YouTube channel linked
   successfully." The channel now appears in the table.

You can link more than one channel if your Client publishes to more than one destination — each
event picks its own upload and (if it restreams) livestream channel independently; see
[[Event-Streaming-Management/Client-Administration/6. Event Configuration Reference|Event Configuration Reference]].

As a Client Admin, you see your own Client's linked channels here and in the channel dropdowns on
Event Configuration and Livestream setup — the same list a system admin sees for your Client. If the
list fails to load, the page says "Couldn't load channels — try again" rather than showing an empty
list; "No channels linked yet" means there genuinely aren't any yet.

# Re-Linking or Removing a YouTube Channel

- If a channel's row shows **"Auth failed — re-link required"** in red, click **Re-link** on that
  row and sign in again — this re-runs the same OAuth flow and just refreshes its tokens; it doesn't
  create a duplicate channel entry.
- Click **Remove** to unlink a channel. The confirmation reads exactly:

  > Remove "{channel name or ID}"?
  >
  > Events using this channel will stop uploading.

# Connecting Slack

1. As a Client Admin, open **Slack Notifications** in the dashboard.
2. Click **Connect to Slack**. This takes you to Slack's own authorization screen — approve it for
   the workspace you want notifications sent to.
3. You're redirected back with the banner "Slack workspace connected successfully. Select a channel
   below."

# Choosing a Slack Channel and Notifications

Once connected, the page shows your workspace name and:

- **Channel** — a dropdown of channels the platform's Slack app has already been invited to. If it's
  empty, the page tells you why: "No channels found. Invite the bot to a channel first, then
  refresh." Invite the app to the channel you want from Slack itself, then come back and refresh.
- **Notify when** — three checkboxes: **"Match uploaded to YouTube"**, **"Clip processing failed"**,
  and **"Clips need review"** (on by default) — sends "1 clip needs review" with the clip's filename
  when a clip is held for review (see
  [[Event-Streaming-Management/Operators/5. Clips, Uploads and Signage#Reviewing Clips|Clips, Uploads and Signage]]).
  These are the only client-facing notification types this platform sends to Slack; other
  operational alerts (upload failures, storage issues, and similar) go to *FIRST* Chesapeake's own
  internal channel, not yours.

Click **Save** (only shown once you've changed something) to apply your channel and notification
choices.

# Removing the Slack Integration

Click **Remove Integration**. The confirmation reads exactly:

> Remove Slack integration?
>
> No further notifications will be sent.

You can always click **Connect to Slack** again later to reconnect.
