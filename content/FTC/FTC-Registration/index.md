This category of the knowledgebase covers the *FIRST* Chesapeake FTC registration site: the web application teams use to register for the season, pay their fees, rank the qualifiers they'd like to attend, and learn where the lottery placed them. It also takes payment for the optional third qualifier and for the District Championship.

Two groups of people use the site:

- **Coaches** sign in to the team portal, pick a team, and work from that team's dashboard. A team's coaches are its lead coach (**LC1 / Coach**), assistant coach (**LC2 / Asst Coach**) and any others the lead coach adds. Fees are paid by card through a Stripe checkout page, or by purchase order.
- **Admins** are *FIRST* Chesapeake staff and volunteers who sign in with an approved Google account. The admin dashboard has tabs for **Overview**, **Teams**, **Events**, **Event Management**, **Lottery**, **Results**, **Awards**, **Email**, **Email Templates**, **Reports** and **Settings**.
